# Community Code of Conduct

VIGIL-IoT contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and participants are expected to help keep SPECTRA-32 respectful, constructive, and safe for technical discussion.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific; critique ideas, evidence, and code rather than people.
- Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Explain security and safety concerns without shaming contributors who correct a mistake.
- Keep discussion within the project's authorized lab scope.
- Respect privacy and do not publish secrets, device identifiers, or personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and requests to test against unauthorized targets are not acceptable.

## Enforcement and reporting

Repository maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report conduct concerns through GitHub's report-content mechanism or the contact method listed on the [VIGIL-IoT organization profile](https://github.com/VIGIL-IoT). Avoid placing private or identifying details in a public issue. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or close contributions when needed to protect participants and the authorized-use boundary.

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, project events, and other spaces where a person represents SPECTRA-32 or VIGIL-IoT.
